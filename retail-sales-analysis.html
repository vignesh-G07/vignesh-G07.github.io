Key insights

  1. Q4 is the peak. October to December bring about 42% of yearly sales, so stock and marketing should be planned for it.
  2. Technology leads, Furniture leaks. Technology is 56% of sales at an 11.9% margin. Furniture is 31% of sales but earns only 2.5% margin.
  3. 20% discounts lose money. Orders with a 20% discount run at a −2.3% margin, against 14.4% with no discount.
  4. South is the strongest region at 34% of sales. East is weakest at 16% and has room to grow.

Recommendations

Cap discounts at 10%, review Furniture pricing and supplier cost, build Q4 inventory early, and test a targeted campaign in the East region.

SQL used

-- Profit margin by category
SELECT category, SUM(sales) AS sales,
       ROUND(SUM(profit)*100.0/SUM(sales),1) AS margin_pct
FROM orders GROUP BY category ORDER BY sales DESC;
-- Monthly sales with running total (window function)
WITH m AS (
  SELECT MONTH(order_date) AS mth, SUM(sales) AS sales
  FROM orders GROUP BY MONTH(order_date))
SELECT mth, sales, SUM(sales) OVER (ORDER BY mth) AS running_total
FROM m;
-- Does discounting hurt profit?
SELECT discount, COUNT(*) AS orders,
       ROUND(SUM(profit)*100.0/SUM(sales),1) AS margin_pct
FROM orders GROUP BY discount ORDER BY discount;
